    Questions

    Before you decide.

    Can you work with the tools we already use?

    Usually, yes. We connect existing spreadsheets, forms, WhatsApp, email and other systems wherever they offer a way to integrate, rather than asking you to start over.

    How are timelines and cost decided?

    They depend on scope, so we do not quote a number before understanding the problem. After discovery you receive a written scope, with the trade-offs explained.

    Do you use AI?

    Only where it adds value. Rules and integrations come first. Where AI is involved, such as extracting data from documents, a person reviews anything that has to be accurate.

    What happens after launch?

    You receive the handover: code, deployment details and documentation. Ongoing maintenance or support can be agreed separately, depending on what your team needs.
