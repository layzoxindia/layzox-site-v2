Privacy Policy.

What we collect on this website, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Layzox India Pvt Ltd. In this policy, “we” means Layzox India Pvt Ltd.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, an email address or a phone/WhatsApp number (or both), the project type you choose and the message you write.
  • WhatsApp, phone or email: whatever you choose to send us when you contact us directly.
  • Your browser: your light or dark theme choice is stored in your own browser (local storage) so the page looks the same next time. It is not sent to us.
  • Fonts: this website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When the fonts load, Google may receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider may record standard request information such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, discuss and deliver work you ask for, keep the website secure, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Who it is shared with

We use service providers to run the site and deliver enquiries to us, for example hosting and email delivery. They process information only to provide those services. If you contact us through WhatsApp, that conversation is also subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics. If we add analytics in future, we will describe it here and use privacy-respecting settings.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond, to manage any resulting work, and for our records. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Legal basis and consent

We process the details you send because you asked us to respond to you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us; we will then stop using your details for that purpose, except where we must keep a record by law.

Where your information is processed

Our hosting and email delivery providers may store or process information on servers outside your country, including outside India. We use providers that protect information with industry-standard security, and we transfer information only as permitted by applicable law.

How we protect it

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Enquiries are sent to a protected server endpoint with spam and abuse controls, and access to enquiry information is limited to the people who need it to reply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct or update it, to delete it, to withdraw your consent, or to stop contacting you. This includes your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where they apply to you, data protection laws such as the GDPR. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal information, write to our grievance contact at contact@layzox.com with the subject “Privacy grievance”. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
