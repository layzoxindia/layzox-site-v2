      FAQ

      Questions before you start.

      Anything else? Send us a WhatsApp and we will answer.

      How much does custom software cost?

      It depends on what the system must do, how many people use it and what it connects to. Send us the brief above and we will ask the right questions, then share a written estimate before you commit to anything.

      How quickly can we start?

      We usually begin with a short conversation about how your work runs today. Once the scope is agreed, the first working version is built in steps you can try along the way.

      Will I own the code?

      Yes. At handover you receive the source code, deployment details and documentation, so you can run it yourself or hire anyone to continue.

      Can you work with the tools we already use?

      Usually, yes. We connect spreadsheets, forms, WhatsApp, email, CRMs and other systems wherever they offer a way to integrate.

      Do you use AI in everything?

      No. Plain rules and integrations come first. We add AI where it clearly helps, and a person checks anything that has to be accurate.

      Do you work with clients outside India?

      Yes. We work remotely with teams around the world and meet over video call at a time that suits you.
