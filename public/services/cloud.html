How we work on it

Four steps, nothing hidden.

Step 1

Review what runs today

We look at how your systems are hosted, deployed and backed up today, and note the risks, such as manual releases, missing backups or single points of failure.

Step 2

Automate deployment

We set up automated, repeatable deployments so releasing a change becomes a routine step instead of a risky manual job, with an easy way to roll back.

Step 3

Add monitoring and backups

We add monitoring and alerts so problems are noticed early, and backups that are tested by actually restoring them, not just assumed to work.

Step 4

Connect data to reporting

We bring data from your tools into one place and build dashboards for the numbers your team acts on, so reports update themselves instead of being compiled by hand.

Questions

Common questions.

Which cloud providers do you work with?

We work with the major cloud and hosting platforms and recommend one based on your needs, budget and existing setup. Your accounts stay in your name.

We already have hosting. Do we need to move?

Not necessarily. We start by reviewing what you have, and often the right step is to improve deployments, monitoring and backups where you are.

How do you make sure backups work?

By restoring them. A backup is only counted as working after we have tested a restore.

Can you build dashboards from our spreadsheets?

Yes. We can pull data from spreadsheets, forms and other tools into one place and build dashboards that update automatically.
