How we work on it

Four steps, nothing hidden.

Step 1

Define users and key journeys

We agree who will use the product, such as customers, staff or partners, and the few journeys that matter most to them, like signing in, placing an order or checking a status.

Step 2

Design and build in working steps

Design and development happen in short steps. Each step ends with a working page or feature on a live link that you can open on your own phone and computer.

Step 3

Check accessibility, speed and security

Before launch we check that pages load quickly on mobile, work with a keyboard and screen readers, and follow sensible security practices for logins, forms and data.

Step 4

Launch and hand over

We launch on hosting you control, monitor the first days closely and hand over the code, deployment details and documentation.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you build marketing websites or web applications?

Both. We build fast marketing sites, and login-based products such as customer portals, booking and ordering systems, admin panels and SaaS products.

Will it work well on phones?

Yes. We design for small screens first and test on real devices before launch.

Can our team update the content ourselves?

Where content changes often, we set up a way for your team to edit it without touching code. We agree what needs to be editable during scoping.

Where will the application be hosted?

On hosting that you own or control. We recommend options based on your needs and hand over all access at the end.
