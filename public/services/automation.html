How we work on it

Four steps, nothing hidden.

Step 1

List the repeated steps

We list the tasks your team repeats every day, such as copying details between tools, sending reminders or chasing approvals, and note how often each one happens and what it costs in time.

Step 2

Automate with rules first

Most repetitive work can be automated with plain rules and integrations, which are predictable and cheap to run. We start there, so the basics work reliably before any AI is involved.

Step 3

Add AI only where it adds value

We use AI for the parts rules cannot handle well, such as reading documents, sorting messages or drafting replies, and only where it clearly saves effort.

Step 4

Keep a human check on what matters

Anything that must be accurate, like amounts, commitments to customers or records of decisions, goes to a person for a quick check before it is final. You decide where those checks sit.

Questions

Common questions.

Do we need AI to automate our work?

Often not. Many tasks are handled well by rules and integrations between your existing tools. We add AI only where it adds clear value, such as reading documents or drafting replies.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

Output that must be accurate is checked by a person before it is used. Automations are logged, so mistakes are visible and can be corrected quickly.

Which tools can you connect?

Spreadsheets, forms, email, WhatsApp, CRMs and most systems that offer an API or another way to integrate. We confirm what is possible for your tools during discovery.

Can our team change the automations later?

Yes. We document how each automation works, and where it makes sense we use tools your team can adjust without a developer.
