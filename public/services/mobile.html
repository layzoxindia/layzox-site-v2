How we work on it

Four steps, nothing hidden.

Step 1

Decide what must work offline

Field teams often work with weak or no signal. We decide early which actions must keep working offline, such as capturing photos, forms or signatures, and how data syncs when the connection returns.

Step 2

Build the key flow first

We build the most important flow first, for example completing a job or placing an order, so you can try the core of the app early on your own devices.

Step 3

Test on real devices

We test on the phones your users actually carry, including older and lower-cost Android devices, with real network conditions rather than only on a fast office connection.

Step 4

Release and iterate

We handle store submission for iOS and Android with your accounts, then improve the app in small releases based on how people use it.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. In most cases we use a cross-platform approach so one codebase serves both, which keeps cost and maintenance lower. We recommend native development only when the app needs it.

Can the app work without internet?

Yes, for the actions you choose. We design offline capture and safe syncing for teams that work on patchy networks.

Who publishes the app to the App Store and Google Play?

We prepare and submit the releases using your own developer accounts, so the apps belong to your business.

Can the app connect to our existing systems?

Usually, yes. The app can read and write data through your existing systems or a shared backend, so web and mobile stay in sync.
