FAQ

Straight answers, before you ask.

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

What does Layzox build?

Custom software and internal tools, AI and workflow automation, web applications, mobile apps, cloud and data systems, and technology consulting. Each is described on its own page.

Where are your clients based?

Anywhere. We work remotely with teams around the world, and meetings happen over video call.

Will I own the code?

Yes. At handover you get the source code, the deployment details and the documentation.

How does a project start?

With a conversation about how your work runs today. We write that down, agree what is built first and what waits, and only then start.

How are timelines and cost decided?

They depend on scope, so we do not quote a number before understanding the problem. After discovery you receive a written scope, with the trade-offs explained.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. We connect existing spreadsheets, forms, WhatsApp, email and other systems wherever they offer a way to integrate, rather than asking you to start over.

Do you use AI?

Only where it adds value. Rules and integrations come first. Where AI is involved, such as extracting data from documents, a person reviews anything that has to be accurate.

How do you handle security and data?

Each user sees only what their role allows, and actions are logged. Tell us early if you handle sensitive or regulated data, because that changes how much checking we do.

What happens after launch?

You receive the handover: code, deployment details and documentation. Ongoing maintenance or support can be agreed separately, depending on what your team needs.

How do I get started?

Use the contact form, call, or message us on WhatsApp. Describe what is slow or scattered today and we will reply with next steps.

Still have a question?

Send it with the form and we will reply with a clear answer.
