Security

Security reporting.

Help us investigate website security concerns responsibly and without exposing other people's information.

Report a concern

If you believe you have found a security issue on the public Layzox website, email contact@layzox.com with the subject “Website security report”. Include the affected URL, a brief description and steps to reproduce the issue safely.

Responsible testing

  • Test only systems you are authorised to assess.
  • Do not access, modify, download or disclose other people's data.
  • Do not perform denial-of-service testing, social engineering or destructive actions.
  • Share only the minimum evidence needed to explain the issue.

What not to send

Never email passwords, OTPs, private keys, access tokens or full customer records. Redact sensitive information from screenshots and logs.

Scope and disclosure

This contact route is for reports about the public Layzox website. It is not a promise of a bounty, a formal vulnerability disclosure programme, a response deadline or a security certification. If your concern relates to a separate customer system, contact its authorised administrator.
