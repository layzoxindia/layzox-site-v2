Questions

Common questions.

Can patient data be kept secure?

Yes. We design role-based access, encrypted connections and audit logs, and follow the data protection rules that apply in your country. Sensitive decisions stay with your staff.

Can it work with our existing billing or lab software?

Usually, yes, where that software offers a way to integrate. We check this in the first conversation.

Do patients need to install an app?

Not necessarily. Booking and reminders can work through a website and WhatsApp, with a mobile app added later if it helps.
