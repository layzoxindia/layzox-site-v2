Engagement models

Pick what fits your stage.

Fixed-scope project

Best when the requirement is clear.

  • Written scope, timeline and price agreed before work starts
  • Delivered in reviewed steps you can try
  • Code, deployment details and documentation at handover

Phased MVP

Best for a new product or an uncertain requirement.

  • Start with the smallest version that proves the idea
  • Decide the next phase with real feedback
  • Budget committed one phase at a time

Monthly retainer

Best for ongoing improvements and support.

  • A set amount of work each month
  • Priorities agreed with you every month
  • Monitoring, updates and small features

Dedicated team

Best for larger or long-running work.

  • Engineers and a delivery lead working on your roadmap
  • Your tools, your rituals, regular demos
  • Scale the team up or down with notice

Questions

Common questions.

Do you publish prices?

Every system is different, so we do not quote a number before understanding the problem. After a short conversation you receive a written estimate with scope, timeline and cost, before you commit.

Which model should we choose?

If the requirement is clear, a fixed-scope project. If it is a new idea, a phased MVP. For ongoing work, a retainer or a dedicated team. We will recommend one after the first call.

Can we switch models later?

Yes. Many projects start fixed-scope and move to a retainer after launch.

How are payments structured?

Usually in milestones tied to delivered, working software. The exact schedule is part of the written proposal.

Tell us what is slowing your team down.

We reply with a practical next step, not a sales pitch.
