How an agent works

From trigger to CRM record.

  1. 01

    A trigger starts it

    A new lead, a missed call, a form submission or an old enquiry you want to revive.

  2. 02

    It holds the conversation

    By voice or chat, following a script and limits you approved. It hands over to a person when your rules say so.

  3. 03

    It writes it down

    Summary, status and next step go to your CRM, and a meeting is booked if it makes sense.

You stay in control

You decide the rules.

01

What it may say

You approve the script and the boundaries. It does not improvise outside them.

02

When it hands over

To a person, on conditions you set, such as a complaint, a price question or a request it cannot answer.

03

What you see

Every conversation, with the transcript and the outcome, so you can check and improve it.

04

When it contacts people

Calling hours, consent and opt-out rules differ by country. We design the flow around the rules that apply to you.

Questions

Before you try it.

Does an agent replace my sales team?

No. It handles first response and follow-up so people spend time on conversations that matter and on exceptions.

Will people know they are talking to an AI?

Disclosure rules differ by country. We design the flow around the rules that apply to you, and we discuss it with you before launch.

Which languages does it support?

We settle this in discovery, based on the languages your customers actually use and what we can verify works well.

Which CRM does it connect to?

Usually whichever one you use, wherever it offers a way to integrate. Tell us what you have.

Tell us what is slowing your team down.

Describe your leads and your CRM, and we will reply with what is realistic.
