Step by step

What happens from first call to handover.

Step 1

Map the process

What we do

We talk to the people who do the work. We record how requests arrive, who touches them, where they wait and which tools are involved.

What you get

A written process map and a clear list of where time is lost.

Step 2

Agree the scope

What we do

We decide what to build first and what waits, and we explain the trade-offs in plain language.

What you get

A written scope you can approve or change before any build starts.

Step 3

Build in steps you can review

What we do

Short build steps, each ending with something you can open and try on a live link.

What you get

Working software at every step, with your feedback applied as we go.

Step 4

Hand over

What we do

We hand over the source code, deployment details and documentation, and walk your team through them.

What you get

Everything needed to run it yourself or hire anyone to continue. Ongoing support can be agreed separately.

Working together

What we need from you.

A good result depends on a few things from your side. None of them are technical.

  • Someone who knows how the work runsA person from your team who can explain the process as it really happens.
  • A decision-maker for each stepSomeone who can approve a step or ask for changes, so work does not wait.
  • Access to the tools involvedAs needed, so we can connect to them rather than replace them.
  • Early notice about sensitive dataIf you handle regulated or sensitive data, tell us at the start. It changes how much checking we do.

Questions

Before you reach out.

How does a project start?

With a conversation about how your work runs today. We write that down, agree what is built first and what waits, and only then start.

How are timelines and cost decided?

They depend on scope, so we do not quote a number before understanding the problem. After discovery you receive a written scope, with the trade-offs explained.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. We connect existing spreadsheets, forms, WhatsApp, email and other systems wherever they offer a way to integrate, rather than asking you to start over.

How do you handle security and data?

Each user sees only what their role allows, and actions are logged. Tell us early if you handle sensitive or regulated data, because that changes how much checking we do.
